2. What ClipForge is

ClipForge is software that ingests long-form video, transcribes and analyses it on your own hardware, cuts vertical clips, and publishes the ones you approve to a video platform you have connected. The expensive work happens on your machine. What we host is a control plane: sign-in, a small amount of job and clip metadata, notifications, and a short-lived copy of a clip so you can review it on a phone.

It is free. There is no subscription, no seat and nothing to buy, and nothing in these terms obliges us to keep operating the hosted parts.

An approved workspace is shared. Every approved member can see every job, clip and publication in it. Do not submit material you are unwilling to show the other members of your deployment.

7. Availability and limits

There is no uptime commitment. The hosted control plane runs on a free tier and may be interrupted, rate-limited or withdrawn. Real limits you should expect:

  • A clip held for review is deleted after five days, and sooner once you have reviewed it.
  • YouTube's own API allowance permits roughly six uploads per day per project, and resets at midnight Pacific time.
  • Nothing progresses while your worker is not running. Jobs wait; they do not fail.

Keep your own copies. The copy of a clip that matters is the one on your machine, and that is by design.
